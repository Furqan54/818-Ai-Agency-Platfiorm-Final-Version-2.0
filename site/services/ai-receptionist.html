Sample call · Marina Dental ClinicTap play to listen (English)
0:00 / 0:55
ReceptionistGood morning, and thank you for calling Marina Dental Clinic. How can I help you today?
CallerHi, yes. I'd like to book a teeth cleaning, if possible.
ReceptionistOf course, sir, I'd be happy to help with that. Would you prefer a morning or an afternoon appointment?
CallerAfternoon, please. Maybe tomorrow?
ReceptionistLet me check the calendar. Tomorrow I have two o'clock, or four thirty. Which one works better for you?
CallerFour thirty is perfect.
ReceptionistWonderful. May I take your name and a mobile number for the booking?
CallerSure. It's Omar Khalid, and my number is zero five zero, one two three, four five six seven.
ReceptionistThank you, Mr. Khalid. You're booked for tomorrow at four thirty with Doctor Sara. I've just sent the confirmation to your WhatsApp. Is there anything else I can help you with?
CallerNo, that's all. Thank you very much.
ReceptionistMy pleasure. We look forward to seeing you tomorrow. Have a wonderful day!

How we work

A clear path from idea to implementation.

  1. 01

  3. 03

  4. 04

  5. 05

What you receive

Clear deliverables.

  • An AI receptionist configured for your business and channels.
  • Conversation flows, FAQs and tone of voice agreed with you.
  • Calendar, CRM or inbox connections where supported.
  • Escalation rules that route people to your team.
  • Testing, handover and usage guidance.

Expected value

Practical business benefits.

  • Fewer missed calls and unanswered messages.

  • Customers get a faster first response.
